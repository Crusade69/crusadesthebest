      About SkillLink

      SkillLink is a revolutionary decentralized platform built for the modern web. At its core, SkillLink enables users to connect their digital wallets and earn cryptocurrency by completing useful, crowdsourced microtasks.

      Whether you're categorizing data, tagging images, summarizing content, or transcribing audio, your efforts are rewarded in real time with $SKL, the platform's utility token. It's the easiest way to turn spare minutes into meaningful value.

      With SkillLink, we're building more than a task hub — we're creating the future of work. One where opportunity is global, decentralized, and powered by blockchain.
